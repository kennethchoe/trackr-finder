TrackR shut down in 2021 and its app stopped working, but the hardware still
functions perfectly. This app talks to it directly over Bluetooth Low Energy,
with no cloud, no account, and no network access of any kind.

The TrackR Pixel is not a proprietary device: it implements the Bluetooth SIG
Find Me Profile, so ringing it is a single one-byte write to the standard
Immediate Alert Service. That means this app is not TrackR-specific — it works
with any Bluetooth tag implementing Immediate Alert, including many inexpensive
"iTag" style trackers, and a surprising number of headphones and wearables.

Features:

* Live scan with a proximity bar and rough distance estimate
* Ring a tag, and silence it again
* Battery level, read while connected
* Local nicknames per device, so identical tags are distinguishable
* Optional leave-behind alert: watch one tag, get notified when it drops out of
  range, and see where you last heard it

Deliberate limitations, stated plainly:

* Signal strength gives you hotter/colder, not a direction. Range is 10-30 m.
* Once a tag is out of range it is gone. TrackR's crowd-sourced finding network
  no longer exists and cannot be recreated. The recorded position is where YOUR
  PHONE was when it last heard the tag, not where the tag is.
* Tile, AirTag and Samsung SmartTag cannot be rung. Their alert commands are
  authenticated against a key held by the owner's account.

Location permission is used only to record where a tag was last heard, and
nothing is ever transmitted off the device. The app requests no network
permission at all.

Tested end-to-end on a single device (Galaxy S25 Ultra, Android 16). Android 8
to 11 take a different permission path that has not been exercised, and no tag
other than a TrackR Pixel has been tried. Reports from other hardware are
welcome on the issue tracker.

If the leave-behind alert stops firing, the cause is usually the manufacturer's
battery manager rather than the app; see the README.
